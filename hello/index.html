Recovered memory: circa 2000

AMACC Programmers Pool · ILOVEYOU virus

More than 25 years ago, I served as lead programmer in an AMACC Programmers Pool effort to develop a countermeasure against the ILOVEYOU virus.

It was one of my earliest encounters with the real-world consequences of malicious code, and helped set my course toward computer security.

There was no GitHub repository or public commit history to preserve our contribution. Just programmers trying to solve a problem that needed solving.

Twenty-five years later, the tools have changed. The curiosity hasn't.

A personal recollection of my involvement, not an independently documented attribution or a claim to have developed the only countermeasure.
