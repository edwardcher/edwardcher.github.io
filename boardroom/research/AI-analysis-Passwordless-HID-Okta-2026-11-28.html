05 / BOARD DECISION

05Considerations for further evaluation

For existing Okta customers requiring hardware-backed passwordless authentication, approve a targeted Crescendo qualification programme before considering additional authentication infrastructure.

  • Confirm exact models, firmware, readers and support boundaries.
  • Demonstrate configuration-only enrolment and authentication.
  • Test FastPass coexistence, attestation and policy enforcement.
  • Simulate lost credentials, recovery and termination.
  • Confirm licensing and vendor escalation paths.
