My manifesto.

From the boardroom to the terminal.

We live in an age where knowledge can be generated, intelligence can be augmented and expertise can be imitated. I find myself thinking more about what defines us as individuals and what it means to preserve our own identity.

What we know is only part of who we are. Equally important is what we do with that knowledge, what we stand for and what we are prepared to take responsibility for.

Throughout my career, I have believed that knowledge gains value through application and that technology should serve a purpose. Trust, on the other hand, takes time to build. It comes from evidence, consistency and accountability.

I believe an executive decision should be traceable all the way down to its architectural rationale and technical evidence.

The boardroom and the terminal are not separate worlds. They are different perspectives of the same problem. I have spent much of my professional life moving between the two, and I continue to find value in understanding both.

This site, edwardcher.online, is an extension of that journey. It gives me a place to think, design, build, challenge ideas and share what I learn along the way. Much of this work comes from experience, but just as much comes from curiosity and the belief that there is often a better way to solve a problem.

My purpose is not to compete against artificial intelligence, but to demonstrate what an experienced human practitioner can accomplish with it, without surrendering intellectual independence. I see AI as a collaborator in this journey, not a replacement for my identity, judgement or responsibility.

Intelligence may be replicated. Experience may be documented. Knowledge may be shared. But I believe identity is something we earn, demonstrate and reinforce through the way we live and the work we leave behind.

This is my way of continuing that journey.

From the boardroom to the terminal.

— Edwardcher
